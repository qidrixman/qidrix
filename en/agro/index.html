IMPLEMENTED PROJECT

Digitization in agriculture

Integration of communication, monitoring and security systems for the agro-industrial complex.

A comprehensive approach to field and machinery management. From building satellite communication channels to implementing crop video analytics and automated fuel accounting.

Solutions
Animation: satellite link, base station, combine and drone sending data to the control room
Step 01. Infrastructure

Organization of communication channels

We provide 100% field coverage with radio signals. This is the foundation for data exchange between harvesters, drones, and the dispatch center.

Global coverage Network access via terrestrial and satellite channels, including Starlink integration for remote areas.
Local networks Building Mesh networks and WiFi zones for uninterrupted communication of special equipment and telemetry transmission.
Animation: a tractor works a geofenced field while telemetry updates live
Step 02. Telemetry

Machinery Monitoring

Navigation blocks integrated into the standard electronic systems of tractors continuously transmit machine status data via GPS and GLONASS.

Parameter control Real-time analysis of fuel level, node temperature, pump operation, and battery charge.
Work management Remote ignition activation and strict control of drivers' work and rest schedules.
Animation: RFID-controlled refuelling with litre accounting and sync
Step 03. Resource Accounting

Fuel and Lubricants Automation

The complex for complete control of petroleum products eliminates the human factor: from receiving fuel at the warehouse to entering the tank of special equipment.

Smart gas stations Fuel dispensing from departmental gas stations in automatic mode without operator intervention.
Synchronization Integration of accounting systems with enterprise corporate platforms (1C, SAP) and issuing waybills.
Animation: a drone surveys the field, zones are coloured by NDVI and a person is detected
Step 04. Analytics

Video Control and UAVs

Smart cameras and drones collect visual data for crop analysis and perimeter protection, saving farm resources.

Field Analysis Yield forecasting, assessment of crop density, and detection of diseases or lack of moisture.
Harvest Management Quality control of processing, recording harvester unloadings, and detecting unauthorized persons on the territory.
